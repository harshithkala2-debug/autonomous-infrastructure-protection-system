The full terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Want to do more than evaluate?

Ask first. Open an issue using the **Permission request** template in this repository
([New issue → Permission request](../../issues/new/choose)) and describe:

* who you are and which organisation you represent;
* what you want to do (for example, a pilot on your infrastructure, integration, publishing a benchmark,
  a commercial licence or collaboration);
* the scope and the time frame.

Until the owner replies in writing with a permission that names you, the answer is **no**.

## Reporting problems

Security issues: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Bugs and evaluation feedback: open an issue. Feedback is welcome.
